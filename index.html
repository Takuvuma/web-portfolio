        About

        I came into identity from the help desk and the network closet — the places where broken access actually shows up. Triaging MFA failures, account lockouts, and unauthorized access attempts for 300 users taught me that IAM is mostly about process discipline: who gets provisioned, who gets reviewed, and who gets removed on time.

        Today I split my time between undergraduate security research (IDS tuning, packet analysis, NIST CSF mapping) and building cloud identity labs in Azure. I'm looking for an IAM Engineer or Identity Analyst role where I can grow into access governance and automation.
